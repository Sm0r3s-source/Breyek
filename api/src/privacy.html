2 versija, galioja nuo 2026 m. lapkričio 19 d.

Į internetą nieko nesiunčia, nebent patys įjungiate dalijimąsi anoniminiais naudojimo duomenimis arba naudojate „Breyek Pro“ (tada tikrinamas licencijos raktas). Jį galite bet kada išjungti, ir tada mūsų serveryje esantys jūsų duomenys ištrinami.

„Breyek“ kuria Lietuvos inžinerijos kolegijos studentų komanda. Duomenų valdytojas – Rokas Smilgius, „Breyek“, Lietuva. Visais klausimais rašykite breyek@protonmail.com.

2. Kas vyksta tik jūsų kompiuteryje

  • Klaviatūra ir pelė – programa žino tik paskutinio paspaudimo ar judesio laiką ir ar tai buvo klaviatūra, ar pelė. Kurie klavišai paspausti, neįrašoma.
  • Ekranas – kad žiūrint vaizdo įrašą laikas būtų skaičiuojamas, programa kas kelias sekundes palygina sumažintą ekrano vaizdą su ankstesniu. Vaizdas lieka tik atmintyje, niekur neišsaugomas ir nesiunčiamas.
  • Pertraukos metu – klaviatūra ir pelė laikinai blokuojamos, o grojantys vaizdo įrašai ir muzika pristabdomi.
  • Statistika – kiek laiko rašėte, naudojote pelę, žiūrėjote ar skaitėte. Ji saugoma aplanke %APPDATA%\Breyek-2.0 60 dienų. Ją galite išjungti ir ištrinti skirtuke „Privatumas“.
  • Nustatymai – kalba, tema ir kiti pasirinkimai saugomi tame pačiame aplanke.

Niekada nesiunčiama – ką rašėte ar matėte, ekrano vaizdai, failai, langų, programų ar svetainių pavadinimai, vardas, el. paštas, vieta, įrenginio duomenys. IP adreso nesaugome.

Kam naudojame – programai tobulinti ir rėmėjams bei reklamuotojams pritraukti. Jiems galime rodyti tik bendrus visų naudotojų skaičius, pvz., „vidutiniškai 2 val. per dieną žiūrima vaizdo įrašų“. Kiek laiko – iki 12 mėnesių, vėliau automatiškai ištrinama.

Privacy policy and terms of use

Version 2, effective 19 November 2026

In short – Breyek runs on your computer and reminds you to rest your eyes. It checks when you type, use the mouse or the screen changes, never what you type or see. Nothing goes to the internet unless you turn on anonymous usage data sharing or use Breyek Pro (then the license key is checked). You can turn it off at any time, and your data on our server is then deleted.

1. Who we are

Breyek is made by a student team at Lietuvos inžinerijos kolegija (Lithuanian College of Engineering). The data controller is Rokas Smilgius, Breyek, Lithuania. For any question write to breyek@protonmail.com.

2. What happens only on your computer

  • Keyboard and mouse – the app only knows the time of your last key press or mouse movement and whether it was the keyboard or the mouse. It never records which keys you press.
  • Screen – so that watching a video counts as use, the app compares a small, scaled-down copy of the screen with the previous one every few seconds. The copy stays in memory and is never saved or sent.
  • During a break – the keyboard and mouse are blocked for a moment, and playing videos and music are paused.
  • Statistics – how long you typed, used the mouse, watched or read. They are kept for 60 days in %APPDATA%\Breyek-2.0. You can turn them off and delete them in the Privacy tab.
  • Settings – language, theme and other choices are kept in the same folder.

3. Data sharing (optional)

It is off by default. If you turn it on, once a day the app sends the following for the previous days:

Random install IDCreated on your computer, not linked to your name, account or device
App informationVersion, language, theme, whether it is the demo
Totals per dayNumber of breaks, minutes of use, and minutes per activity: typing, mouse, full screen, watching videos, reading

Never sent – what you type or see, screenshots, files, window, app or website names, your name, email, location or device details. We do not store your IP address.

Why we use it – to improve Breyek and to attract sponsors and advertisers. We may show them combined figures for all users only, such as "on average 2 hours a day are spent watching videos". We never sell or share the data of a single install, and we do not build profiles of individual people. The sponsors shown during breaks are the same for everyone.

Legal basis – your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by turning sharing off. The app then asks the server to delete all data of that install.

Where it is stored – in a Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) database in the Western Europe region. Cloudflare is our data processor. It sees your IP address only to deliver the request, and transfers are covered by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long – up to 12 months, then it is deleted automatically.

4. Breyek Pro subscription

  • Price – €1.99 a month or €15.99 a year. The free version stays free and keeps every feature that protects your eyes.
  • Payment – payments, invoices and VAT are handled by Polar (polar.sh). Polar is the merchant of record and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We never receive your card details.
  • License check – once a day the app sends your license key to Polar's server (api.polar.sh) to check that the subscription is active. Only the key and our account ID are sent. The key is stored on your computer.
  • Cancelling – you can cancel at any time with the "Manage subscription" link. Pro keeps working until the end of the paid period. Without internet, Pro keeps working for up to 7 days.
  • Refunds – Polar's refund policy and your EU consumer rights apply.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its use, and withdraw consent. The quickest way to delete everything is to turn sharing off in the app. Otherwise write to breyek@protonmail.com. You can complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt) or the authority in your country.
